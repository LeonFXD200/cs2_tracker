💡 Market Tips

📈
Major Events = Sticker Spike

Team stickers from upcoming majors rise in price weeks before the event as demand builds.

📦
New Case Releases

When Valve drops a new case, older case skins and keys often shift in value — watch the market closely post-announcement.

🔧
Game Updates & Patches

A buff to a weapon increases demand for its skins. Check HLTV and Valve patch notes alongside price movements.

📅
Weekend Effect

Player counts rise on weekends. More players = more demand, which can temporarily push popular skin prices up.

🔢
Float Value Matters

Low-float Factory New skins can be worth significantly more than average condition. Use CSFloat DB to research.

⚠️
Skinport vs Steam Prices

Skinport prices are typically 10–20% lower than Steam Market due to different fee structures. This tracker uses Skinport data.
